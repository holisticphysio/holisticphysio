### ⚠️ On-Page Issues

#### Issue #4: Keyword Cannibalization on Condition Pages [MEDIUM PRIORITY]
**Status:** NEEDS WORK

**Issue:** Pages like `pain.html`, `stress.html`, `fatigue.html` all target similar search intent ("acupuncture for [condition] Brisbane") with overlapping messaging  
**Impact:** Medium - could fragment ranking ability across related pages  
**Evidence:**
- pain.html, sports-injuries.html, work-injuries.html likely competing for "physio + injury" keywords
- fatigue.html, stress.html, anxiety-and-depression.html competing for wellness keywords
- No unique keyword targeting per page documented

**Fix:** Create keyword map document; assign unique primary keyword to each page. Examples:
- pain.html → "chronic pain physio Brisbane"
- sports-injuries.html → "sports injury recovery Brisbane"
- work-injuries.html → "work injury physio Brisbane"
- Internally link from one to another with context ("Related: sports injuries")

**Priority:** 2 (High)  
**Estimated Effort:** 1-2 days

#### Issue #5: Thin Unique Content on Condition Pages [MEDIUM-HIGH PRIORITY]
**Status:** NEEDS WORK

**Issue:** Condition pages (pain.html, fatigue.html, stress.html, etc.) are ~2000-2500 words, but much is boilerplate (clinic features, opening hours, nearby suburbs). Original first-hand content may be 600-800 words.  
**Impact:** Medium-High - condition pages are high buyer-intent targets but lack depth to outrank competitors  
**Evidence:** Word counts solid, but ~40% of content is boilerplate (clinic features, location info, booking CTA)  
**Comparison:** Service pages (physiotherapy.html, acupuncture.html) at 3000+ words with deeper content

**Fix:** Expand unique content per condition with:
- Specific symptoms checklist (e.g., "Red flags: sharp pain when lifting")
- Case study or patient example (anonymized)
- Recovery timeline for that condition
- Why this condition is common in Brisbane (climate, lifestyle factors)
- Integration notes: "How acupuncture helps stress-related neck pain"
- Common misconceptions about the condition

**Target:** Add 500+ words of unique, first-hand content per page  
**Priority:** 2 (High)  
**Estimated Effort:** 2-3 weeks (10 priority condition pages)  
**Pages to prioritize:** pain.html, stress.html, fatigue.html, sports-injuries.html, work-injuries.html

#### Issue #6: Missing FAQ Schema [LOW-MEDIUM PRIORITY]
**Status:** NEEDS WORK

**Issue:** FAQ section exists on homepage but no FAQ schema markup (JSON-LD)  
**Impact:** Low-Medium - missed opportunity for rich snippets in search results  
**Evidence:** FAQ section on homepage with questions, but no `schema.org/FAQPage` or `schema.org/Question` markup  
**Fix:** Wrap FAQ content in proper FAQ schema (template provided below)  
**Priority:** 3  
**Estimated Effort:** 1 day

**Schema Template:**
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "Do I need a doctor's referral?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "No, you can book directly..."
      }
    }
  ]
}
```

**Impact:** Low - could help with rich results if schema present  
**Fix:** Add Service or MedicalTherapy schema to service pages  
**Priority:** 4 (nice-to-have)

## Reference: Barefoot Physiotherapy's Location Page Strategy

**How they do it:**
1. Location-specific title/H1 (e.g., "Physiotherapy Annerley")
2. Explicit single location statement: "Our clinic is located in Tennyson, a suburb close to Annerley"
3. Localized intro & distance messaging (varies: "6 min" vs "12-15 min" vs references to "The Gabba")
4. Unique FAQ questions tailored to each location
5. Context-specific content (e.g., Woolloongabba page emphasizes sports)
6. **All pages link to identical services** (not what we implemented)

**What we did differently (better for SEO):**
- ✅ Added explicit location notes
- ✅ Varied featured services section per location
- ✅ Prevents identical link patterns across pages
- ✅ Maintains single location clarity
